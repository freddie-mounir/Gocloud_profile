Our Story

Company Milestones

2015

A Legacy of Innovation

In 2015, GoCloud emerged with a clear vision to revolutionize the technology landscape in Egypt. Starting with a dedicated team of innovative professionals, we recognized the growing demand for comprehensive digital solutions in the MENA region. Our initial focus was on delivering customized software solutions that would help local businesses embrace digital transformation.

2017

Expanding Our Horizons

As technology continued to evolve, so did we. We expanded our service offerings to include a wide range of solutions — from IT services and cloud computing to data analytics and artificial intelligence — establishing ourselves as a multi-domain technology partner.

2023

ELITE Platform Launch

ELITE — our comprehensive medical insurance management system — was born. Designed specifically for the healthcare insurance industry, it streamlines the entire insurance lifecycle: policy creation, claims processing, provider network management, and regulatory compliance. Built with cutting-edge technology, ELITE empowers insurance companies to deliver efficient, transparent, and customer-centric services.

2025

ERP & CRM Era

We expanded into comprehensive Odoo ERP & CRM implementations tailored for diverse business sectors. Our solutions empower real estate companies, gold investment firms, and medical facilities — all through one integrated platform. Today, as an official Odoo partner, we continue to drive digital transformation across the region.
