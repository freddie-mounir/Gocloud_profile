How Odoo CRM Is Transforming Gold Trading Companies

2026-04-01 GoCloud Team

Gold trading companies face unique challenges unlike almost any other commercial sector, from real-time price volatility to the need for strict sales controls and continuous visibility into the performance of many branches at once. This is where GoCloud’s specialized Odoo CRM for gold trading comes in.

Restricted Sales Policies

In gold trading, sales activities cannot be left without strong governance. The customized Odoo CRM provides:

  • Strict sales limits — define daily, weekly, and monthly ceilings for each salesperson and branch with instant alerts as limits are approached.
  • Multi-level approvals — any deal above a defined threshold requires manager or executive approval before completion.
  • Dynamic pricing linked to global gold prices — direct market price linkage with adjustable margins for each product category.
  • Full audit trail — track every price or policy change, including who changed it and when.

Sales Targets

The system supports target setting at multiple levels:

  • Individual targets — for each salesperson based on experience, territory, and historical performance.
  • Branch targets — monthly and quarterly targets for every branch with live comparison to actual performance.
  • Product targets — track sales across product categories such as bars, jewelry, and coins to ensure balanced distribution.

Progress toward targets is displayed visually with color indicators: green when targets are achieved, orange when close, and red when behind plan.

Branch Dashboard

One of the strongest features is the branch dashboard, which gives management an instant, comprehensive view of every branch:

  • Scheduled and completed tasks — pending, completed, and overdue tasks per branch with performance ratios.
  • Customer status — new, interested, not interested, no response, and completed customers with live figures.
  • Total weights — monitor the total weight of traded 21K gold in grams for each branch.
  • Deposits and withdrawals — display cash movements with net profit or loss per branch.
  • Team activity — meetings, conversations, calls, and follow-up tasks by branch.
  • Agent activity indicator — compare active agents with total agents to measure real branch activity.

Reports and Analytics

The system provides advanced analytical reports covering:

  • Daily and weekly performance reporting per branch
  • Branch-to-branch performance comparisons
  • Sales trend analysis against gold price fluctuations
  • Compliance reports for sales policy adherence and violations

Why GoCloud?

The GoCloud team has deep experience in tailoring Odoo for gold trading and precious metals businesses. We understand the complexity of this sector and deliver purpose-built solutions that account for:

  • The unique nature of gold-market pricing
  • Regulatory and compliance requirements
  • The need for speed and data precision
